# Bundled data

| File | Contents | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `water_absorption_segelstein1981.txt` | Pure-water absorption coefficient (1/cm) vs wavelength (nm), 10 nm - 10 m | D. J. Segelstein, "The complex refractive index of water", M.S. thesis, University of Missouri-Kansas City (1981). File as distributed by Oregon Medical Laser Center: https://omlc.org/spectra/water/data/segelstein81.txt |
| `water_absorption_popefry1997.txt` | Pure-water absorption coefficient (1/cm), 380-700 nm | R. M. Pope & E. S. Fry, "Absorption spectrum (380-700 nm) of pure water. II. Integrating cavity measurements", Appl. Opt. 36, 8710-8723 (1997). File from https://omlc.org/spectra/water/data/pope97.txt |

Pope & Fry is used for 380-700 nm (more accurate in the blue, where older data overestimate
absorption); Segelstein everywhere else.
