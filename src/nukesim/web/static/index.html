      How to use nukesim

      1. Pick a scenario at the top, or set the burst yourself on the left (yield, above or below the water, how far).
      2. Add things to the sea: fish, whales, dolphins, turtles, divers and boats. In the technical view, drag them to move them.
      3. Click one to see what happens to it: blast, hearing, burns, radiation and fallout, each with the numbers behind it.
      4. Use the timeline at the bottom to play the event from the flash to weeks later.
      5. Switch to Realistic to watch it as it would look: ocean, sky, fireball, mushroom cloud and waves.
      6. Basic / Expert switches how much detail you see. Expert shows every criterion, source and caveat.

      All numbers come from published sources (Glasstone & Dolan, U.S. Navy, IAEA, ICRP and others) and carry their limits. This is an educational tool, not a substitute for detailed engineering models.
